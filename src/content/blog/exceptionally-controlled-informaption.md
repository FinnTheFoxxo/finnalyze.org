Around June of 2013, the media began posting classified documents disclosed by former National Security Agency contractor Edward Snowden. Since then, only a small subset of the documents from a larger pool that Snowden had allegedly taken have surfaced to the public. Specifically, two of these documents discuss programs maintained in a secrecy compartment called "Exceptionally-Controlled-Information" or ECI for short.  ECI doesn't stand on its own; rather, it's embedded inside the Special-Intelligence (SI) compartment; a compartment inside of a compartment. [Formerly known](https://www.nsa.gov/portals/75/documents/news-features/declassified-documents/nsa-css-policies/NSACSS_PM_1-52_20210108.pdf#%5B%7B%22num%22%3A30%2C%22gen%22%3A0%7D%2C%7B%22name%22%3A%22XYZ%22%7D%2C69%2C106%2C0%5D) as Communications Intelligence (COMINT), SI covers Signals Intelligence (SIGINT) related information. ECI was created to protect knowledge that should require additional secrecy beyond what traditional levels can provide. As such, ECI programs require individual clearance, with a mandatory indoctrination and briefing for a specific program.

The two documents mentioned earlier are from different points in time. The [12th of September, 2003 listing](https://theintercept.com/document/nsa-exceptionally-controlled-information-listing-12-september-2003/) has an original document date of the 24th of February, 1998. However, the document appears to have been updated over time, as it states the ECI listing is as of the 12th of September, 2003. Additionally, the document is classified as TOP SECRET and bears the markings X1 and NOFORN. [X1](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2010/06/28/2010-15443/classified-national-security-information#sectno-reference-2001.26) is a legacy handling marking relating to declassification that means a document is exempt from automatic declassification on the basis that the document could reveal a U.S. confidential source or specific agency relationships. NOFORN specifies that the document is not releasable to foreign nations, with the exception of applicable portion marks, as demonstrated in the document. The [second document](https://theintercept.com/document/eci-control-authorities/) is much more recent; originally dated January 8th, 2007, it has been updated up until January 2013. It bears no X1 or NOFORN marking. In fact, this document is classified CONFIDENTIAL and bears the REL marking, authorizing it to be released to Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom, and New Zealand; the four main associates of the US that make up the Five Eyes intelligence alliance.

Additionally, while the 2003 listing gives a declassification date of X1, the 2013 listing gives an actual declassification date of January 1st, 2038.
